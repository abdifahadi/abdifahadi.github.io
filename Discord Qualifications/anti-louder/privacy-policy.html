Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for ANTI LOUDER. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data when you invite our Discord bot to your servers. ANTI LOUDER is a premium security and moderation tool designed to protect voice channels from malicious audio (earrape) and text channels from profanity.

2. Data We Collect

To provide our advanced moderation services effectively, we may collect and process the following data:

  • Server and User IDs: We temporarily process Discord IDs to track moderation actions, apply timeouts, and enforce security protocols.
  • Message Content: We read message content in real-time strictly to detect profanity or malicious links. Message content is processed in memory and is not stored permanently in any database.
  • Voice Audio Data: The bot processes live voice streams to detect extreme volume or software manipulation (e.g., VST Plugins). Audio data is analyzed in real-time. In certain premium security configurations, server administrators may opt-in to save short, encrypted audio clips of rule violations purely for moderation evidence logs.

3. How We Use Your Data

The collected data is used exclusively for:

  • Enforcing server rules automatically (timing out, muting, or banning violators).
  • Detecting prohibited audio manipulation software.
  • Improving our threat-detection AI and algorithms.

4. Data Storage and Retention

ANTI LOUDER prioritizes your privacy. Moderation logs are tied to User IDs and retained only as long as necessary to enforce server security. Server administrators can request complete data deletion at any time by removing the bot and contacting our support team.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal data or server information to outside parties. All data is strictly utilized for the internal operations of the bot.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via our official Discord support server.
